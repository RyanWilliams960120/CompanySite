About the Dracoin Cross-Chain Staking Platform

Dracoin Cross-Chain Staking is an existing Web3 staking platform being upgraded by DracoinLabs. The platform is designed to provide users with a unified experience for staking, rewards, wallet management and blockchain transactions across Ethereum and Solana.

We are building an extensible architecture that can support additional blockchain networks in the future without requiring the core platform to be rebuilt.
